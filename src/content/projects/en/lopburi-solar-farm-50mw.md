## Customer Story · 2025

**Customer:** Solar developer (NDA) · **Location:** Lopburi Province · **Capacity:** 50MW · **Delivered:** Q2 2025

Advanced Grid designed and installed a TOU energy meter, power quality analyzer, and communication box system for a 50MW solar farm project — cutting monthly reconciliation time by 70% and delivering data to the utility's tariff system with 100% accuracy.

### The Problem the Customer Needed Solved

The 50MW solar farm project in Lopburi Province is one of the projects selling power to the Electricity Generating Authority of Thailand (EGAT), which sets strict data-reporting requirements. Every inverter station has to report kWh, kVAR, power factor, and power quality data back to the utility's central system in real time, 24/7 — a single error at one point could result in a full month's revenue being withheld.

Previously, the customer's team had to walk each station to read meters by hand, calculate TOU figures in Excel, and prepare a report for the utility every month — a process that took more than 5 business days and carried a real risk of manual data-entry errors.

### Advanced Grid's Approach

Advanced Grid surveyed the site and redesigned the entire system architecture to meet the utility's tariff metering standards, built primarily on Class 0.2S equipment with a communication system designed for a solar farm's outdoor environment.

- Installed SECURE Prometer 100 meters (Class 0.2S) at the grid interconnection point and the main inverter stations.
- Deployed CEWE ProQ 100 power quality analyzers at the point of common coupling, to monitor power quality to IEC 61000-4-30 Class A.
- Designed and built outdoor IP54-rated meter boxes and communication boxes with lightning protection.
- Built a communication path from RS-485 → fiber backbone → 4G/cellular failover, feeding both the customer's SCADA system and the utility.
- Carried out commissioning and a witness test together with the utility's engineers — passed on the first attempt.

### The Result

Once the system went live, the customer's team no longer had to walk the site to read meters each month. Data from every point now flows automatically into SCADA and the utility's system. Preparing the monthly report now takes half a day, and every data submission to the utility has passed review.

Advanced Grid also provides annual calibration and 24/7 support to keep the system running throughout the 25-year PPA contract term.

### Products Used in This Project

- **SECURE Prometer 100** (Energy Meter · Class 0.2S) — 18 units installed at the grid interconnection point and main inverter stations.
- **CEWE ProQ 100** (Power Quality Analyzer · IEC 61000-4-30 Class A) — 2 units installed at the point of common coupling.
- **Annual calibration service** — yearly calibration of every meter, with certificates accepted by the utility.
