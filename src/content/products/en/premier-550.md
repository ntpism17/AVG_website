A high-accuracy industrial meter for tariff billing and energy trading applications, supporting both LV and HV work, covering measurement at MV feeders and distribution transformers, with power quality monitoring for distribution-network asset analysis.

Supports multiple communication channels — RS 485, RS 232, and a variant with a built-in modem — with a choice of DLMS or MODBUS RTU protocol, plus an optical port for on-site readout where a WAN is unavailable.

Manufactured by SECURE (UK/India), deployed in more than 80 countries worldwide.
