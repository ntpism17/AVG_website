A Class 0.2S, 3-phase TOU meter for interconnection points requiring high accuracy in tariff metering and energy trading transactions. Supports 4-quadrant energy measurement, tracking generation, transmission and load across the distribution network.

Field-replaceable, hot-pluggable communication modules that can be remotely configured give flexible connectivity to AMR/AMI/SCADA systems, with built-in IEC 61850 support for substation automation.

In production use at 18 units across the main grid interconnection and inverter station points of a 50MW solar farm project.
