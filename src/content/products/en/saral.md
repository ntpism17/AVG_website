A compact, direct-connected single-phase meter for residential and small-shop metering, including Net Billing applications that require recording both import and export energy.

Delivers stable measurement across a wide range of field conditions, with revenue-protection features. Reads via an IEC 62056-21 port, with optional BLE for readout via a mobile app.
